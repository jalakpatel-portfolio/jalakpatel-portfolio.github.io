Case 04 · Owner channel strategy · Swad Cooking Institute

Building a content strategy for a newly launched owner channel

The owner’s personal channel was handed over with three videos and about 1,500 subscribers. From November 2024 to October 2025, I worked on its content strategy, publishing direction and selected video shoots, with on-set support as the work developed.

Context

A channel with only a few videos

Chef Sneha Thakkar Recipes had launched as a separate personal channel. At handover, it had about 1,500 subscribers and three videos. The case focuses on the content and production decisions rather than an unverified end-state total.
