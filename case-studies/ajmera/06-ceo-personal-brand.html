Case 06 · CEO personal brand

CEO personal brand

Founder-facing content so leadership presence supported the same B2B trust goals as the corporate surfaces.

Context

Leadership is part of the brand

Buyers often decide based on who they believe they are dealing with. A quiet founder profile left that question unanswered online.

Problem

Corporate only is incomplete

Company pages show the organisation. Founder channels show accountability and continuity - especially in regional wholesale.

Strategy

Align founder voice with business goals

Publish founder-facing posts that reinforce product, process, and reliability without turning every update into a hard sell.

Evidence

Founder content stream

Founder-facing posts and personal brand pieces were planned as a separate stream from corporate updates, so the CEO voice stayed distinct from the company page.

A public post from the founder’s profile

Takeaway

Personal brand supports B2B trust

When the founder channel is intentional, it becomes another distribution and credibility surface - not a vanity account.
