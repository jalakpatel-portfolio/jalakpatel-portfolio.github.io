About the company & role

B2B textiles, built for Tier 2 and Tier 3 India

Ajmera Fashion is a B2B textile manufacturer and wholesaler focused on sarees and traditional wear. The commercial model is not “sell fabric online.” It is help small retailers and first-time entrepreneurs start a clothing business — guidance on what to stock, mentoring, and wholesale supply. YouTube and social were trust and discovery layers; inventory sales remained the core outcome.

This was my first digital marketing role. Scope grew from day-to-day uploads into systems across language and product channels: regional product maps, title formulas, and search language that matched how people in each state actually shop and search.

Audience niche and creative strategy

The primary audience sat in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities — buyers and aspiring retailers who respond to local taste, not generic metro fashion language. The same industry later repeated at Jalan; both required thinking in regional niches rather than one national feed.

Strategy leaned into that niche on purpose: chunky, high-contrast thumbnails that read clearly on mobile, and desi / regional keywords (for example Paithani or Sambalpuri-style search terms instead of only “silk saree”) so discovery, trust, and engagement stayed aligned with how those audiences already decide what to watch and what to stock.
