Case 02 · Facebook Gujarati groups

Groups as a Distribution Surface

Facebook Gujarati groups used as a practical channel for reach where organic page posts were not enough on their own.

647 → 14,473 followers recorded
Organic group distribution

Context

Where the audience already gathered

Many of the buyers and browsers Ajmera needed were already active inside Gujarati-language Facebook groups. The work focused on those dedicated group surfaces rather than page-only reach.

Problem

Page reach was not the only surface

Treating Facebook only as a company page limited distribution. Groups required different packaging, timing, and respect for group norms.

Strategy

Use groups without spamming them

Content was adapted for group context: clearer product framing, language fit, and posting patterns that matched how those groups actually read posts.

Results

Another door into the same catalog

The recorded follower count moved from 647 to 14,473. Growth was organic through Facebook groups. The case focuses on the distribution decision rather than claiming a per-post result.

Takeaway

What this showed

For regional brands, the highest-leverage Facebook work is often outside the page graph: in the groups where the language community already spends time.
