Case 01 · Ajmera Fashion

A language is not an audience

How regional product preferences shaped the title formulas and search terms used for each audience.

Telugu subscribers: +32.4% (Dec 2022–Jun 2023)Tamil subscribers: +31.6% (Dec 2022–Jun 2023)

Context

Regional channels needed their own system

Language alone was not an audience. Each region needed its own product map, title formula, and search phrases.

Problem

Generic keywords lose regional search

Uploading the same product framing across regions treated language as a simple translation job. Buyers in each market search by local product terms, not generic labels.

Strategy

Product map, title formula, search phrases

The work was to build a repeatable system so regional content was not a generic translation of the main channel.

Result

Two regional channels showed subscriber growth

From December 2022 to June 2023, Telugu subscribers grew by 32.4% and Tamil subscribers by 31.6%.

Takeaway

Customisation, not translation

Regional growth came from matching how people actually search in each market, not from translating the same post into another language.

Work output

Selected output - regional product hooks

Public regional product videos used as hooks for each language audience (YouTube landscape ratio).
