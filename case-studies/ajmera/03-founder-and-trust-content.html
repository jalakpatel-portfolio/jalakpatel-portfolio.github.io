Case 03 · Ajmera Fashion

Trust, from real people

Product videos show what a wholesaler sells. These tracks answered the question buyers actually had: can I do this, and can I trust this seller?

11 founder topics planned9 public videos3 content tracks

Context

Buyers decide on trust before price

Ajmera Fashion’s buyers are small retailers and first time entrepreneurs. Many of them are deciding whether to start a clothing business at all, and whether this supplier is the right one to start it with.

Problem

A saree on a table does not answer the real question

A product video does not tell a first time buyer whether they can run a clothing business, or whether the seller will still be there next season. Without an answer to that, the product never gets a fair look.

Strategy

Three tracks, one question

Alongside the product videos, the team ran three tracks that each answered the buyer’s real question from a different side.

Educational track
Brand ambassador
Founder series
  • Educational track. Videos about the business opportunity itself, so a viewer could picture themselves as a seller.
  • Brand ambassador. A real person to associate with the brand.
  • Founder series. The founder speaking directly about business, investing and starting small.

Execution

What I did

Results

The evidence is the work itself

The tracker holds channel level numbers, not per video results, so this case makes no performance claim for these videos. It also claims no audience results for the founder’s personal pages. What it shows is the plan and the finished work, public and playable above.

What this showed

Trust is content too

For a B2B seller, the product is only half the content. The other half answers whether the buyer can do this and whether the seller can be trusted. Planning that second half on purpose, with its own tracks and its own topic list, was the lesson I took from this part of the job.
