Case 04 · Jalan Mega Mart

Festival calendar and offline marketing

A calendar of regional festivals and offline touchpoints so stock, creative, and outreach stayed aligned with buying moments.

Context

Festivals drive textile demand

Jalan sold textiles to buyers across India. The calendar was not limited to one state's festivals. It covered dates that mattered for stock and buying behaviour in different regions.

Problem

Missed dates mean missed demand

Without a shared festival calendar, creative and offline promotion landed after the buying window or without matching inventory.

Strategy

Plan the year around buying moments

Map festivals and regional peaks, align content and offline activity to those windows, and keep a record of what was promoted when.

Results

A usable operating calendar

The festival system gave the team a repeatable way to plan content and offline work against real demand windows. Specific enquiry records are supplied where the archive supports them.

Between uploads

YouTube community posts

When a video was not live or a new upload was not out yet, community posts kept the audience engaged, invited replies, and brought people back to the channel.

Takeaway

Calendar before creative

When festival timing is fixed first, content and offline work support the same commercial moments instead of competing with them.
