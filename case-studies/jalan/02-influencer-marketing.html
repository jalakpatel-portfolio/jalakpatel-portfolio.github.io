Influencer marketing coordination

Context

Coordinating creator activity

The work involved finding people who fit the product, agreeing terms, briefing them, and keeping booking decisions organised. Creators were selected for regional language reach and product fit rather than metro-only following size.

Briefs covered product angle, shooting style, and delivery windows so the final videos stayed usable across the channel network. Booking records and budget notes were kept so the team could see what had already been committed and what remained open.
