About the company & role

From a group textile strength into a Surat B2B build

Jalan Mega Mart sits in the same broad textile industry as Ajmera, with a different chapter: a group pivot from the Banaras-side strength of Shalini Fashion into a Surat-based manufacturing-to-wholesale operation. When I joined, the company was only a few months old. Marketing was not an inherited machine — channels, pages, influencer relationships, and team rhythm had to be built.

The official title was Social Media Manager. In practice the scope became marketing operations: channel and page network, influencer coordination, creative direction, tele-calling support, festival and offline planning, and budget allocation across those lanes.

Shared niche with Ajmera — different stage of the problem

Like Ajmera, the real customer sat in Tier 2 and Tier 3 markets — retailers and buyers who respond to regional product language and clear commercial trust, not metro-only creative. Strategy carried the same niche logic forward: chunky thumbnails that hold attention on mobile, and desi / regional keywords and framing so reach and engagement matched how those audiences already search and decide.

Ajmera taught systems on an existing portfolio. Jalan required the same audience instinct while standing up the function itself.
