Case 07 · Company LinkedIn

Company page with its own editorial lane

Company page strategy, publishing cadence, and engagement trends alongside the founder voice.

Context

Institutional voice needs a system

The Cinekorn company LinkedIn page had to represent the organisation without mirroring the founder feed. Cadence, topics, and proof of engagement mattered - including how distribution infrastructure and licensing windows actually work.

Problem

Company pages drift without ownership

Without a clear publishing plan, company pages either go quiet or become pure announcement boards.

Strategy

Cadence and industry content

Set a publishing rhythm and mix institutional updates with distribution-relevant industry content so the page stays useful beyond press releases - infrastructure readiness, rights windows, and how a distributor sequences value across territories.

Selected posts

Company voice: infrastructure and rights architecture

These posts carry the institutional lane: how readiness turns a regional hit into a pan-India event, and how licensing windows - not "the file moved" - explain why titles appear and disappear on streaming platforms.

Infrastructure

Kantara and the 14-day dubbing question

With parallel dubbing readiness, a regional hit becomes pan-India; without it, the same film stays regional. Infrastructure speed is a pre-greenlight question.
