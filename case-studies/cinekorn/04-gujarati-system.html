Case 04 · Gujarati multi-format distribution

One film, multiple release formats

How Gujarati catalogue assets were distributed through full releases, serialised parts, Shorts, and social cuts - with each format planned for a different watching job.

Context

A film can have more than one useful release format

Cinekorn Gujarati serves a regional Gujarati-speaking audience. Full releases, serialised parts, Shorts, and Facebook cuts each match different habits: complete viewing, return visits, discovery, and phone-first scroll. Treating every surface as a copy of the same upload wastes formats the same title can support.

Problem

A single upload path leaves reach on the table

When a title only goes out as a full film, the parts, Shorts, and social cuts that could keep the same story working across platforms never get built into the plan.

Strategy

Match the format to the job

Full release for complete viewing and SEO. Serialised parts for return traffic and playlist depth. Shorts for discovery. Facebook cuts for alternate reach and recovery windows. Tenure performance is tracked against that plan - not mixed into lifetime catalogue totals.

Titles that already travel well across platforms - notably Vikram Thakor catalogue films - are amplified through this packaging system rather than reinvented. Playlist analytics become the operating surface for how parts hold attention over time.

Results

Takeaway

Distribution is a packaging decision as well as a publishing decision

Format choice is part of the strategy. When full, parts, Shorts, and social cuts are planned together, the same title can work across platforms without treating every surface as a copy of the same upload.
