About the company & role

Film distribution at portfolio scale

Cinekorn Entertainment is a Mumbai-based film distribution, production, and content syndication company. The business spans theatrical, satellite, and OTT pathways across Bollywood, South Indian, regional (including Gujarati and Marathi), and Hollywood titles - with a large digital footprint on YouTube and social.

As Senior Social Media Manager I own digital movie content strategy, multi-platform distribution and revenue operations, and LinkedIn for both the company and the founder. The work is not one channel playbook; it is a system across accounts that must stay honest about legacy catalogue versus tenure publishing.

Audience is not the same on every channel

Cinekorn Gujarati serves a regional Gujarati-speaking audience. Format, language, and release design (full film, parts, Shorts, Facebook cuts) follow how that audience actually watches - including phone-first and serialised habits - rather than copying a national Hindi feed.

Cinekorn Movies and Cinekorn Cineplex pull a mixed audience that comes for South and Bollywood catalogue viewing. Strategy there is catalogue operations: what to publish, how to title and package, and how to separate lifetime archive performance from work shipped during tenure.

Telugu and Kannada run under tighter catalog and quality constraints; judgment is about what is worth activating, not forcing volume. LinkedIn (founder and company) is a professional narrative surface - different intent from entertainment YouTube entirely.

One licensed film can feed several of these audiences when distribution is designed on purpose. That is the strategic difference between uploading content and operating a content system.
