Resume & JD System

Share a Job Description below. You stay on this page - no need to open another site.

Step 1

Honest Assessment

After you submit, I review the role against my real experience and reply with a clear fit answer: interview yes/no, strengths, and gaps.

Step 2

Tailored Resume

If the fit is useful, I prepare a resume written for that Job Description using portfolio work only - no invented numbers.

How it works: Fill the form on this page (company, job title, JD, your email). I receive it by email, then send you Step 1 and - if useful - Step 2. This is not an instant auto-score; it is a careful human review so the answer stays honest.

I spent significant time building this portfolio and system so hiring decisions can be clearer for both sides. If this page or any part of my work was useful, the simplest next step is a short conversation. Even a brief message that you reviewed the work is appreciated. Thank you for your time.
